About

I'm a comic artist working in the manga tradition: character design, sequential pages and finished inks. My original series is Shinobi: Undercover.

I work traditionally, pencil first and then pen, brush and ink on paper, and I film the process. The inking sessions go out as short timelapses and longer real-time videos.
