I'm a comic book artist and character designer. Ooz Studio Ink is my studio, and this channel is where I share the work. Everything here is drawn and inked by hand.

You'll see the whole process, not just the finished piece. Short clips go up three times a week. Every Friday there's a LongDraw: one drawing from the first rough lines to the last stroke of ink. It's mostly anime and manga characters, plus my own designs.

I also take commissions for character designs and comic art. Details are on my site.

New Shorts: Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 12 PM ET
LongDraw: every Friday at 12 PM ET
