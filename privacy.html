Privacy Policy

Ooz Reels Bot · Last updated November 7, 2026

Ooz Reels Bot is a private tool run by Ooz Studio Ink to edit and publish Ooz Studio Ink's own short videos to its own Instagram, YouTube, Threads and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in or collect data from anyone other than the account owner.

What the bot accesses

Only the Ooz Studio Ink accounts its owner connects:

  • Basic account details (account name and ID) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Videos the owner sends to the bot, and the edited versions it creates.
  • Performance statistics for those posts (for example views, watch time, likes and shares), used to decide which edits work best.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos on the owner's behalf, only after the owner approves them or allows automatic posting.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to create, schedule and publish the owner's videos and to measure how they perform. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared. Post statistics and the posting queue are stored in the project's private configuration. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Ooz Reels Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time from Google account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Instagram's Apps and Websites settings, or TikTok's app permissions. Revoking access stops the bot from reading or posting, and stored tokens are deleted.
