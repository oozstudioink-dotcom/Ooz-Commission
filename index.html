What to know before booking

  • Deposit 50% non-refundable to secure your spot in the queue, with the remainder due on delivery.
  • Turnaround & queue Turnaround begins once I've received both your deposit and all reference material. Queue position follows deposit-received order, not request order.
  • Revisions 2 rounds included with every package. Please request them within 7 days of receiving a proof.
  • Usage rights Personal use is included by default. Commercial rights are available as an add-on, agreed on before work begins.
  • Delivery High-resolution PNG files delivered via a shared Google Drive link. Source files available on request for an additional fee.
  • Cancellation If you cancel before I've started, your deposit is refunded minus a small administrative fee. Once work has begun, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • What I don't offer NSFW content, satanic imagery, or anything outside my stated artistic boundaries.
