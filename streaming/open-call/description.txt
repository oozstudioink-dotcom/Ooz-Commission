FULL DESCRIPTION / ARTIST STATEMENT
I'm a comic artist and character designer, and Ooz Studio Ink is my one-person studio. Everything I
make starts on paper: pencils first, then ink, all by hand. My work lives in bold black and white,
heavy shadows, expressive brushwork and dynamic action, drawing on manga, anime and classic comics.

I create comic pages, character designs, pin-ups and illustrations, from fan-favourite characters to
original concepts built from scratch. I also share the process: I stream my drawing sessions live and
post weekly drawing videos, so people can watch a piece go from the first rough lines to the final ink.

I'm open to commissions, collaborations, exhibitions, comic and zine anthologies, and character
design work for independent creators, games and brands.
